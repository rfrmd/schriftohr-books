THE SWISS FAMILY ROBINSON (Wyss), CHAPTERS 10 TO 18 (the end)
Content review, 2026-11-03. Read in full (C10 to C18, about 2,500 lines). Nothing has been changed.
Line numbers refer to swiss-b.txt. Every CURRENT text below is copied from the file and was checked to occur there.

------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------
Counts: 25 SOFTEN (7 of them marked optional), plus 1 probable typo, 0 CUT, 4 NOTE, about 40 LEAVE.
  Race and ethnic terms: 17 SOFTEN (3 optional; 9 of them in C17, most in the canoe scene)
  Minced oaths: 3 SOFTEN (2 "Thank Heaven", 1 optional)
  Theology and Scripture: 3 SOFTEN (1 optional), plus 1 probable typo
  Animals: 2 optional SOFTEN, 1 NOTE

Strongest items:
1. C17, the canoe scene (lines 2132 to 2164): Fritz, his face stained dark, is taken for "a tall and muscular
   savage", "the swarthy native", "a tribe of blacks"; Jack shouts "you black son of a gun"; the family asks why he
   "turned blackamoor", and washing off the stain turns him "once more into a civilized being". The scene works
   without any of it. About ten small changes, and the chapter heading.
2. "Hottentot" twice (C13 line 1130, C17 line 2106). The word is now taken as a slur. "The herdsmen of the Cape".
3. "Savages" elsewhere: C10 291, C15 1488 ("a naked savage"), C15 1704, C18 2390.
4. "Thank Heaven!" twice (C12 1026, C17 2170). The father says "thank God" everywhere else, so "Thank God" is his own voice.
5. C10 213: constant work "is found to constitute the main element of happiness" (WSC 1). And C16 1774 puts
   Psalm 90 in David's mouth; the Psalm is "A Prayer of Moses the man of God".

On the theology as a whole: this half is sound. Providence is named as God's hand again and again (976, 1714, 2392,
2478, 2488); prayer is submission ("Thy will be done", 1524) and thanksgiving (1528), never a lever; the father prays
alone to "the Almighty Father" (1828); family worship is kept (1050, 1400); his parting charge is "duty toward God"
and holding "fast to the faith" (2498). Nothing teaches salvation by works, luck in place of Providence, or deism.
What it lacks is Christ. He is not named once in C10 to C18. That is a lack, not an error, and no rewrite is proposed.

HOW TO READ THE PROPOSALS
  SOFTEN  change a few words so the story stays and the problem goes
  CUT     remove a sentence that can go with nothing lost
  NOTE    leave the text, and say a word about it in About This Edition
  LEAVE   fine in context

THEOLOGY AND SCRIPTURE
------------------------------------------------------------
C10 line 213 (constant work)
  CURRENT  is found to constitute the main element of happiness.
  SOFTEN   is, under the blessing of God, a great part of our happiness in this life.
  WSC 1: man's chief end is to glorify God and enjoy Him. Work as "the main element of happiness" is the one place
  the father's good sense slides into a creed of its own. Small change; the teaching on diligence stays.

C16 line 1774 (opening of the ten-years chapter)
  CURRENT  “We spend our years as a tale that is told,” said King David.
  SOFTEN   “We spend our years as a tale that is told,” said Moses, the man of God.
  Psalm 90:9; the Psalm's title is "A Prayer of Moses the man of God". A factual slip, not doctrine, but a family
  reading the Psalms would notice.

C16 line 1964 (the pet ape)
  CURRENT  long since gathered to his fathers)
  SOFTEN (optional)  long since dead and buried)
  Scripture's phrase for the death of the patriarchs, used as a joke about a monkey. Mild; your call.

C12 line 1088 (probable typo, not a content change)
  CURRENT  it might be the will of God to deprive them easily of their parents
  SOFTEN   it might be the will of God to deprive them early of their parents
  I expect "early" is the author's word; I have not checked it against the source edition. Check before changing.
  The thought itself (a father preparing his sons for God's providence) is sound. WCF 5.1.

NOTE  The Lord's Day is never named in this half. Daily family worship is (1050 "our accustomed devotions",
  1400 "evening devotions"), and no Sunday work or play is shown, so nothing teaches against the Fourth Commandment.
  No change; one line in About This Edition only if you want it for the whole book.

NOTE  Christ is not named in C10 to C18. The piety is Providence, prayer and thanksgiving, all of it sound. No change.

LEAVE (sound, and worth keeping):
  455  bones of whales and birds show "the wisdom and goodness of the great Creator" (natural revelation, WCF 1.1;
       it does not replace Scripture)
  976  "a beneficent Providence scatters plants of wild melons" (Providence feeding the creature, WCF 5.1, Ps 104)
  1124 "the chief thing of all, is God's goodness in bringing you safe back to us"

  1714 the barrier "which defended, in so providential a manner"
  1780 to 1784 thanks to God for ten years; a pious, industrious home as the forming of character (upbringing,

ANIMALS
------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE  C10 lines 45 to 67, the apes at Woodlands: birdlime, dogs and clubs, "at least forty apes lay mangled and dead".
  It protects the farm, and the book's own response is right: "we looked with a shudder", "the boys began to be quite
  sad". Keep it. One optional softening, where the dogs' attack is dressed up as justice:
  CURRENT  who now rushed to the attack of the unfortunate monkeys, as though burning with zeal to execute justice upon desperate criminals.
  SOFTEN (optional)  who now rushed to the attack of the unfortunate monkeys.

C11 line 565, Grizzle and the boa (the father describing it to the boys as it happens)
  CURRENT  closer and more tightly he curls his crushing folds, the bones give way, he is kneading him into a shapeless mass.
  SOFTEN (optional)  closer and more tightly he curls his crushing folds.
  The mother takes Franz indoors (567); this is the most graphic sentence read aloud to small children. The scene,
  the danger and Ernest's epitaph all stay.

NOTE  C15 lines 1638 to 1646: Fritz poisons the apes at Prospect Hill. Under your misdeed rule, keep it: the story
  learns from it in his own words, "I wished I had not found the poison", and they bury the dead. The father had
  planned the same (1166). LEAVE the text.

LEAVE: pigeons by torchlight (25) and the whale, seals, herring, peccaries, bears, boar (food and stores); the
condor and vulture shot at the bears' den (1056); the dogs on the ostrich, with "This sight grieved us" (968);
the hyena, lion and lioness (in defence of flock and family); the turtle "condemned and executed" (465, a joke);
Jack's matches in the bees' nest, which he pays for (1144); the albatross struck and then nursed back (1894, 1908);
the traps "made that they should kill the rats at once, for I could not bear the idea of animals being tortured"
(1290), a good lesson in itself.

OTHER THINGS CHECKED, ALL LEAVE
------------------------------------------------------------
- Jenny Montrose in a midshipman's dress (2174 to 2276): the secret is kept with care, she sleeps aboard or in
  her bower apart from Fritz, and the engagement waits on her father's consent (2500). Wholesome.
- "the generality of her sex" (2252), "accomplishments suited to her sex" (2260): period, and the passage praises her.
- "Fritz and Jack stripped" (343) and canvas trousers only (365) for the whale work: plain and unremarkable.
- Wine and mead at table (2186, 2304, 2448): temperate.
- "NO ADMITTANCE FOR ASSES." (684): the old word for a donkey, and a joke on fools. LEAVE.
- Fritz lets the brothers think the spare seat is for one of them (1936), and the father covers his absence
  (2088, "he had a fancy to explore more of the coast", which is partly true). A kept surprise, soon told. LEAVE,
  or NOTE if you want the father's words exact.
- "Fit abode for demons" (916) and "like demons" (1634): boys' exaggeration.
